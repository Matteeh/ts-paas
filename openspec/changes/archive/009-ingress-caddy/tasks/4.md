## Context

Contract: "Running hook" and "Deploy result" in the `deployments` delta.

This is the only edit to the deployment engine, and it stays small. Add an optional `onRunning` field to `DeployOptions` in `src/deployments/engine.ts`. In `deploy`, await it with the deployment record after the `running` status change and its progress event, and before `replacePrevious`.

Today everything after `createDeployment` runs inside one `try` whose `catch` force-removes the new container and marks the deployment `failed`. A rejected hook must not reach that `catch`: the new deployment stays `running` with its container, `replacePrevious` does not run, and `deploy` rejects with the hook's own error object. One way is to leave the `try` with the running deployment and call the hook and `replacePrevious` after it.

Do not touch `src/deployments/stop.ts`, `src/deployments/types.ts` or any other file. The engine knows nothing about ingress. The three existing deploy test files must pass unchanged; they are frozen.

Tests use `FakeRuntime`, `FakeClock` and `openStore(":memory:")`, and advance the clock past the health window as `tests/deploy-engine.test.ts` does. Copy its helpers; do not import from that file.

## Ports

```ts
// src/deployments/engine.ts (addition to DeployOptions)
onRunning?: (deployment: Deployment) => Promise<void>;
```

## Acceptance
- [ ] On a redeploy of `web` over running deployment A, the hook is called once with B's record, whose status is `running`; at that moment A is still `running` in the store and A's container inspects as `running`.
- [ ] After the hook resolves, A is `replaced` and its container is gone, and the progress events are `pending`, `pulling`, `starting`, `running` for B, then `replaced` for A.
- [ ] When the hook rejects with `new Error("push failed")`, `deploy` rejects with that same error object; B and A are both `running`, both containers are running, and no `failed` or `replaced` status change was recorded.
- [ ] A first deploy with a hook calls it once, and without a hook `deploy` behaves as before.
- [ ] A deploy whose container fails its health window, or whose pull fails, never calls the hook and resolves with a `failed` deployment.
- [ ] `tests/deploy-engine.test.ts`, `tests/deploy-health.test.ts` and `tests/deploy-stop.test.ts` pass unchanged.
